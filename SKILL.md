# IPTV Affiliate SEO

Rank IPTV affiliate offers on Google and get cited by AI answer engines. This skill is opinionated: it treats IPTV as a **reviewer vertical, not a link-farm vertical**, because Google's 2024 Site Reputation Abuse update and every major platform's spam filter now punish the old link-drop playbook. The pieces that survive are the ones that read like a real person tested three providers and picked one.

## When to use

- Writing an IPTV review, listicle, or comparison post
- Choosing which platform to publish IPTV content on (Reddit vs Medium vs Quora vs YouTube)
- Structuring an IPTV post so ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google AIO quote it
- Fixing an IPTV post that got removed, de-indexed, or ignored
- Building an affiliate funnel around an IPTV offer without getting the account banned

## When not to use

- Piracy tooling, credential sharing, DRM circumvention — out of scope
- General SEO for non-IPTV verticals — use `parasite-seo` or `seo-strategy`
- Paid ads for IPTV — different discipline, not covered here

## What actually ranks an IPTV post in 2026

Google's 2024 **Site Reputation Abuse** policy targets "third-party content published primarily to manipulate rankings." IPTV is one of the most-flagged verticals because it's saturated with thin affiliate posts. The posts that survive share four traits:

1. **Named testing method.** "I subscribed to three providers for 30 days" — with dates, device, connection type. Not "we tested." Not "our team."
2. **A real limitation on the recommended service.** Every honest review has at least one negative. A post with zero negatives reads as an ad and gets filtered.
3. **Comparison structure.** Two or more named services, side by side, in a table. Single-provider posts are affiliate spam; comparisons are buyer guides.
4. **A specific device + region frame.** "On Firestick 4K Max, UK broadband, three Premier League weekends" beats "works on all devices worldwide."

Miss any of the four and the post is dead on arrival — regardless of keyword density, backlinks, or platform authority.

## Platform ranking for IPTV

| Rank | Platform | Why | Posting shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reddit (`r/IPTVReviews`, `r/FireStickHacks`) | Highest AI-citation weight. ChatGPT and Perplexity quote Reddit threads verbatim for "best X" queries. | 800–1,500 word comparison post, no link in body — link only in comments after engagement. Account needs 30+ days and 50+ karma. |
| 2 | Medium | DA 95. Long-form comparison posts rank page 1 in 2–3 weeks. Canonical-safe. | 1,800–2,500 words. Story-led opening. One link near the end. |
| 3 | Quora | Long-tail queries with huge view counts. AI engines cite Quora. | 400–700 word answer to a specific question. One link at the end. |
| 4 | YouTube | IPTV buyers search video first. Descriptions and transcripts are indexed and cited. | 5–8 min screen recording. Title carries the keyword. Description has the links. |
| 5 | GitHub (README in `awesome-iptv` style list) | Very high AI citation, low competition. Not a traffic source — an entity signal. | Curated list entry or README. |
| 6 | LinkedIn Pulse | B2B only (reseller infra, CDN economics). Skip for consumer offers. | 1,000-word thought-leadership. |
| 7 | Generic gaming/PC forums | Low crawl frequency for commercial queries. Google may index, AI engines won't cite. | Last resort. |

**Rule of thumb:** three strong posts on ranks 1–3 beat ten weak posts on ranks 5–7.

## Keyword clusters that convert

Stop targeting "iptv" — it's dominated by piracy-adjacent sites Google is actively de-ranking. Target the buyer's actual query:

| Cluster | Example queries | Intent | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Research — best-of** | "best iptv subscription 2026", "best uk iptv 2026" | Buyer ready | Medium |
| **Problem-aware** | "iptv buffers during premier league", "iptv freezes at 3pm saturday" | Buyer ready | Low |
| **Device-specific** | "best iptv for firestick uk", "iptv for lg c3", "iptv for apple tv 4k" | High intent | Low |
| **Comparison** | "provider A vs provider B iptv", "cheapest iptv that works" | Decision stage | Very low |
| **Replacement** | "iptv alternative to sky sports", "iptv instead of now tv" | High intent, high value | Medium |
| **Technical** | "iptv epg not working", "iptv m3u playlist setup" | Trust-building, low commercial | Low |

Place the primary keyword in title and first 100 words. Secondary keywords go in section headers, not stuffed into paragraphs.

## E-E-A-T shapes that survive Google's filters

For IPTV specifically, "Experience" and "Trust" carry the review. "Expertise" is less important — a genuine 30-day user beats a "streaming infrastructure expert" with no subscription.

**Experience signals to include:**
- Device model (Firestick 4K Max, not "Firestick")
- Connection (UK FTTP, 900 Mbps, not "fast internet")
- Test window ("three Premier League weekends, Feb 2026")
- What failed ("buffered twice during Liverpool-Arsenal, ~8 seconds each")
- Screenshot of the EPG or player (real one, not stock)

**Trust signals to include:**
- At least one negative about the recommended service
- At least one positive about the competitor you don't recommend
- Affiliate disclosure ("links are affiliate; I paid for all subscriptions tested")
- Last-updated date visible at top

**Skip entirely:**
- "We are a team of experts"
- "Our proprietary testing methodology"
- Star ratings without a source
- "As an AI" — kills the post instantly on every platform

## GEO structure for AI citation

AI engines extract **passages**, not pages. To get quoted by ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AIO:

1. **Answer the query in the first 40–60 words.** Not a preamble. The literal answer.
   > **Best IPTV subscription for UK 2026:** [Provider A] — it was the only service that held 4K during three Premier League weekends without buffering on Firestick 4K Max in Manchester.

2. **Follow with a comparison table.** AI engines lift tables cleanly.
   | Provider | Price | Peak-hour reliability | Device support | UK sports |

3. **Then the full review.** Long-form below the answer block. AI engines that need depth will read it; engines that need the short answer already got it.

4. **Use plain H2 headers as questions.** "Which IPTV works best on Firestick?" — AI engines match query-to-header and lift the paragraph below.

5. **One claim per paragraph.** Multi-claim paragraphs get mangled in extraction.

## Link strategy

- **Never link the affiliate in the body of a Reddit post.** Link goes in a comment after the post has 10+ upvotes. Otherwise: ban.
- **Medium: one link, near the end.** Canonical the post to Medium itself. Affiliate link via redirect or the platform's native link shortener.
- **Quora: one link at the very end.** Not mid-answer.
- **YouTube: links in description, not spoken.** Spoken URLs get flagged as monetization violation.
- **Cross-link parasite posts to each other** — Reddit thread links to Medium review links to Quora answer. Builds a citation network. Does not link to the affiliate site from every node — that's the spam signal.

## Output format

When asked to produce an IPTV post, ship:

1. **Platform choice** and why (one line)
2. **Target keyword** and 2–3 secondary
3. **Title** (platform-optimized, under 60 chars for Google indexing)
4. **Answer block** (40–60 words, AI-citable)
5. **Body** (1,200–2,000 words, E-E-A-T shaped, comparison table included)
6. **Affiliate disclosure** (one line, visible)
7. **Link placement** (specific: "link in comment after upvotes" or "one link in final section")

## What kills the post

- Zero negatives about the recommended provider → looks like an ad → filtered
- "Best" claim with no comparison → affiliate spam signal
- No device specified → no E-E-A-T → no ranking
- Link in body of Reddit post → ban
- Same post copy-pasted across platforms → duplicate content → de-indexed
- Keyword in every paragraph → over-optimization → filtered
